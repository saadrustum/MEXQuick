# Market-Making Levels

![Market-Making Levels](<.gitbook/assets/image (47)>)

To ensure the long-term stability of the market-making ecosystem and provide users with a clear growth pathway, MEXQuick has introduced a Market-Making Tier System. This system is aligned with the platform’s VIP Tier Framework and is designed to reward both engagement and contribution.

Overview

The tier system integrates two key metrics:

* Direct Referrals — a fixed participation requirement.
* Personal Contribution — measured by either staking quota or daily trading volume, whichever is higher.

By fulfilling these criteria, users can gradually unlock higher tiers and earn greater rewards within the market-making ecosystem.

Upgrade Rules

Each tier requires a minimum number of direct referral users. Only valid users are counted toward this requirement.

Definition of a valid user

A valid user must meet at least one of the following conditions:

* Staking amount ≥ 100 MUSD, or
* Personal daily trading volume ≥ 100 USDT

Personal contribution standards

To advance to the next tier, users must meet one of the following:

* Staking amount ≥ the corresponding tier standard, or
* Personal daily trading volume ≥ the corresponding tier standard

The MEXQuick Market-Making Tier System encourages active participation, network growth, and sustained liquidity contribution. By combining referral activity with personal performance, users can progress.
