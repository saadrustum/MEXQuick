# Revenue Model

![Revenue Model](<.gitbook/assets/image (41)>)

MEXQuick’s revenue model is built upon real trading activity and multi-market liquidity management, ensuring that all returns have a clear and traceable economic foundation. Users stake MUSD as collateral to participate in the market-making pool, while the platform generates income through multiple revenue channels. All settlements and distributions are conducted in the MQT platform token.

### Sources of Revenue

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Trading Fees

* All transactions involving Event Contracts, Rhythm Contracts, and Ticket Contracts include a trading fee.
* A portion of this fee supports platform operations and risk management, while the remainder contributes to market-making revenue.
* Purpose: To provide a stable, predictable, and sustainable stream of cash flow for the ecosystem.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Entry Fee Accumulation

* Ticket Contracts employ a fixed entry fee mechanism.
* When a user’s prediction is incorrect, the entry fee is used to settle the difference, with the remaining portion retained as platform revenue.
* This accumulated capital is ultimately injected into market-making profits, strengthening system stability.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Cross-Market Arbitrage

The AI market-making engine continuously monitors major exchanges such as Binance, OKX, and Superp, executing real-time arbitrage strategies, including:

* Cross-Exchange Arbitrage: Exploiting price discrepancies between platforms to buy low and sell high.
* Triangular Arbitrage: Capturing inefficiencies among currency pairs such as BTC/USDT, ETH/USDT, and BTC/ETH.
* Funding Rate Arbitrage: Leveraging differences between perpetual swap and spot/futures funding rates.

Feature: Proven quantitative trading logic, validated in traditional and digital markets alike.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Volatility Capture

Short-term derivatives markets naturally exhibit high volatility, creating both risk and opportunity. MEXQuick employs a Delta Neutral + Gamma Scalping strategy to capitalize on these movements:

* Maintain directional neutrality while dynamically balancing exposure.
* Buy low and sell high during short-term fluctuations to capture volatility premiums.

The result is an “anti-fragile” market structure where higher volatility can translate into higher profitability.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

***

### Profit Distribution Model

MEXQuick uses a 70/20/10 allocation model to maintain equilibrium between user incentives, platform stability, and token value.

| Allocation | Recipient          | Purpose                                                                                                             |
| ---------: | ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
|        70% | Market Makers      | Distributed as daily MQT rewards based on staked MUSD. Users can withdraw or reinvest for compound growth.          |
|        20% | Risk Reserve Fund  | Used to hedge exposure and replenish liquidity during extreme market conditions, maintaining ecosystem stability.   |
|        10% | MQT Buyback & Burn | Regular repurchase and burn of MQTs to create a deflationary token economy, enhancing long-term scarcity and value. |

***

### Form of Income

* MUSD (Molding Certificate): Serves as the entry token for market making, pegged 1:1 with USDT to ensure price stability.
* MQT (Income Token): All platform revenues and staking rewards are distributed in MQT. Users can:
  * Exchange MQT for USDT via the Smart Market, or
  * Trade it peer-to-peer on the UID Market.

Additional Features:

* Fee deductions
* Stake binding
* Governance and voting rights

***

### Settlement Frequency

Profits are settled daily and distributed in real time. Users have full flexibility to withdraw or reinvest their earnings, benefiting from the compound interest effect over time.

MEXQuick’s revenue ecosystem is built around four primary modules:

* Trading Fees
* Entry Fee Accumulation
* Cross-Market Arbitrage
* Volatility Capture

This structure ensures traceable, sustainable, and diversified income sources. Combined with the 70/20/10 profit allocation model, it achieves a long-term balance between user rewards, platform risk management, and token value appreciation.

Ultimately, users stake MUSD to participate in institutional-grade market making, earning returns in MQT — a token that can be freely traded or reinvested — forming a robust, transparent, and self-sustaining financial closed loop.
