# Overview

![Overview](<.gitbook/assets/image (29)>)

An event contract is a short-term price prediction product based on a binary options mechanism. Users simply determine whether the price of the underlying asset (e.g., BTC/USDT) will be higher or lower than the current price at the end of a specified period, and receive a fixed percentage return based on the prediction result.
