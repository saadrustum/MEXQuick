# Risk Management

![](.gitbook/assets/image)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Fixed risk

The investment amount represents the maximum loss.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Unified pricing mechanism

All users in the same round share the same opening and settlement prices.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fair and transparent multi-source pricing

Aggregates prices from multiple exchanges, updates every 500ms, and eliminates outliers.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Non-betting model

The platform does not profit from user losses; profits and losses are settled through matching or liquidity pools.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Position limit management

Limits are set on individual users and the platform's total holdings to prevent concentrated risk.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Extreme market protection

New positions can be suspended during periods of unusual volatility, and liquidity pools are used for hedging to ensure settlement security.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
