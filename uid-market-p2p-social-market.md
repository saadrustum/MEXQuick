# UID Market (P2P Social Market)

{% step %}
### Definition and Purpose

![UID Market image](<.gitbook/assets/image (71)>)

**UID Market** is MEXQuick’s decentralized, social secondary market designed to merge financial interaction with social identity. Unlike traditional centralized matching systems, UID Market revolves around each user’s **UID identity**, enabling peer-to-peer (P2P) asset transfers, social engagement, and direct transactions without intermediary control.

Core Concept: Transform trading from a purely financial act into a **social and identity-driven experience**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Function and Mechanism

**P2P Matching**

* Users initiate trade requests using their unique UID and complete transactions upon mutual confirmation.
* The platform only facilitates **settlement and risk management**, never acting as a counterparty.

**Identity-Based Trading**

* Every UID can be linked with **tiers, NFTs, or equity cards**, forming a distinctive “**Social Asset ID**.”
* A UID represents not just a trading account — but a verified **financial and social identity** within the ecosystem.

**Social Gameplay & Engagement**

* **UID vs. UID Revenue Competitions:** Direct peer-to-peer profit challenges.
* **Team vs. Team Leaderboards:** Competitive ranking systems for collaborative trading groups.
* **Guild Mode:** Enables users to form **teams and communities** driven by UID connections, fostering viral social growth and engagement.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Rates and Rules

**Asset Transfers**

* The initiator pays a **5% handling fee**, distributed as:
  * **70%** to platform revenue, supporting infrastructure sustainability.
  * **30%** to the **Risk Fund Pool**, strengthening system resilience.

**NFT / Equity Card Circulation**

* Fixed transaction fees apply.
* A portion of each fee is used to **repurchase and burn MQT**, directly enhancing token scarcity and value.

**UID Battle / PK Mode**

* Both parties agree on a **challenge amount** before initiating a match.
* The platform collects a **10% handling fee**, part of which is directed into a **Reward Pool** for ongoing community incentives.

**Pricing Mechanism**

* Prices are **set by the initiator** and confirmed by the counterparty.
* The platform focuses solely on liquidation, maintaining a **lightweight, non-order-book architecture** for efficiency and transparency.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Advantages

* **User-Friendly:** Simple P2P operations eliminate the need to understand order-book mechanics.
* **Decentralized:** The platform never takes a counterparty position, ensuring neutrality and transparency.
* **Fair & Transparent:** Fixed fees and open rules eliminate hidden costs.
* **Socially Driven:** Every transaction doubles as an **interaction**, turning UID into a dual carrier of **identity and value**.
{% endstep %}
