# Design Principles

![Design Principles](<.gitbook/assets/image (68)>)

## Allocation Strategy: 35% Community, Strict Vesting, Market Stability

* **Community Priority**: 35% is allocated to user incentives to ensure that users are the primary beneficiaries of value.
* **Ecosystem Driven**: 20% is used for ecological expansion to promote the actual use and consumption of MQT.
* **Long-term Constraint**: The team and investors strictly lock up and release linearly to avoid short-term arbitrage.
* **Market Stability**: Liquidity and reserves ensure token price stability and reduce the risk of external shocks.
