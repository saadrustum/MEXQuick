# How Retail Traders Can Become Market Makers

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (52)>)

MEXQuick allows qualified users to participate in market-making activities and contribute to platform liquidity. Any verified user who meets the platform’s requirements can follow the steps below to join the market-making pool.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Complete Account Registration and KYC

To ensure platform security and regulatory compliance:

* Create your MEXQuick account.
* Complete the **Know Your Customer (KYC)** verification process.

Once your KYC is successfully approved, your account becomes eligible for market-making participation and fund management.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Submit a Market-Making Application

After KYC verification:

* Go to the **“AI Market-Making”** section on the platform.
* Submit your **Market-Making Application** for review.
* Provide the required basic information to assess your **risk level** and **eligibility**.

{% hint style="info" %}
Applications may be approved or rejected depending on factors such as your **location**, **regulatory requirements**, or **account status**.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Prepare MUSD Liquidity (For Market-Making Use)

Market-making operations on MEXQuick require **MUSD** as the pledged asset.

* Exchange **USDT → MUSD** through the **Smart Market** on the platform.
* The converted MUSD will be used exclusively within the market-making pool.
* MUSD does not participate in direct trading — this ensures that **market-making** and **trading ledgers** remain separate for better transparency and risk control.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Stake MUSD and Join the Market-Making Pool

Once your application is approved:

* Select the amount of **MUSD** you wish to stake.
* The system will automatically add your funds to the **market-making pool**, supporting:
  * Long and short order matching
  * Liquidity provision
  * Risk management

{% hint style="info" %}
The entire process is **fully automated**, requiring **no manual intervention**.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Redeem Anytime

You have full control over your staked assets:

* You may initiate a **redemption** at any time to withdraw your staked MUSD.
* Redeemed MUSD can then be exchanged back to **USDT**.
* Redemption rules or timeframes may vary depending on **market conditions** or **risk control policies**.

{% hint style="warning" %}
Always refer to the latest information displayed on the **platform page** for the most accurate redemption details.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
