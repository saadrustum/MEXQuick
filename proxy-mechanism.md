## 💹 Examples of Earning Through Referrals

{% step %}
### Market Making Yield Example

**Scenario:** You invite friends and earn a percentage of their market making profits across three levels.

#### Step-by-Step Breakdown

* **Level 1**
  * You invite **Friend A**.
  * **A earns:** 1,000 MQT/day from market making
  * **You earn:** 2% of A’s earnings = **200 MQT/day**
* **Level 2**
  * **A invites Friend B**.
  * **B earns:** 1,000 MQT/day
  * **A earns:** 2% of B’s earnings = 200 MQT/day
  * **You earn:** 1% of B’s earnings = 100 MQT/day
* **Level 3**
  * **B invites Friend C**.
  * **C earns:** 1,000 MQT/day
  * **B earns:** 2% of C’s earnings = 200 MQT/day
  * **A earns:** 1% of C’s earnings = 100 MQT/day
  * **You earn:** 1% of C’s earnings = 100 MQT/day

**💰 Total Earnings:**\
With just these three levels, you can earn an **additional 400 MQT/day**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Trading Volume Rebate Example

**Scenario:** You earn a rebate based on your friends’ trading volume.

#### Step-by-Step Breakdown

* **Invite Friend D**
  * **D trades:** 10,000 USDT/day
  * **First-level rebate:** 0.8% → You earn 10,000 × 0.8% = **80 USDT/day**
* **Team Trading Volume Bonus**
  * If your **team's cumulative trading volume reaches 100,000 USDT/day**,\
    you receive a steady rebate of **800 USDT/day**

{% hint style="info" %}
💡 Tip: Consistently growing your team can lead to significant daily rebates.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}
