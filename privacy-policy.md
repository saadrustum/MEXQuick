# Privacy Policy

## 🔒 MEXQuick Privacy Policy

MEXQuick is committed to respecting and protecting user privacy. The platform strictly complies with applicable data protection laws and ensures the secure and lawful use of all personal information.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Data Collection

* **Account Information:** Email address, mobile number, and real-name details (KYC).
* **Transaction Information:** Deposits, withdrawals, trading history, and asset balances.
* **Device & Usage Information:** Login IP, device model, operation logs, and Cookies data.
* **Interaction Information:** Wallet addresses, screenshots, and social account details submitted during platform tasks or events.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Data Usage

* To provide, maintain, and enhance platform services, including transactions and customer support.
* For identity verification and anti-fraud checks under **KYC/AML** compliance.
* To facilitate rewards, events, and user interaction (e.g., leaderboards or mystery boxes).
* With user consent, information may be used for marketing and product promotion.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Data Sharing

* Shared only when necessary with payment processors, KYC service providers, security auditors, or strategic partners.
* May be disclosed to legal or regulatory authorities when required by applicable laws or court orders.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Data Security & Retention

* Encrypted storage, role-based access controls, and real-time intrusion monitoring safeguard user information.
* Data is retained for the minimum period required by law; other data is anonymized or securely deleted once no longer needed.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### User Rights

Users have the right to:

* Access, correct, or delete their personal data.
* Withdraw consent for data processing (without affecting lawful processing prior to withdrawal).
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

{% hint style="info" %}
For privacy-related concerns, users may contact the platform through the Privacy Protection Email provided on the official website.
{% endhint %}
