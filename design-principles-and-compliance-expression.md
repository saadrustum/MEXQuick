# Design Principles and Compliance Expression

![Design Principles and Compliance Expression](<.gitbook/assets/image (64)>)

* Clear stratification: USDT (transaction layer) / MUSD (market-making layer) / MQT (income and equity layer) have clear division of labor.
* Mechanism precedes commitment: "mechanism protection and value capture" replaces rigid commitments and fixed income statements.
* Emphasis on endogenous and deflationary dynamics: output is tied to real contribution, consumption is linked to ecological applications, and deflation is coordinated with repurchase.
* Gradually open governance: on the premise of controllable risks and improved user maturity, the governance boundaries of MQT will be steadily expanded.
