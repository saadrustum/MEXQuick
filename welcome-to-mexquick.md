# Welcome to MEXQuick

![MEXQuick image](<.gitbook/assets/image (2)>)

## What is MEXQuick

MEXQuick is a next-generation Web3 platform for global users, focused on derivatives trading and AI market making. We specialize in short-term trading, AI-driven liquidity, dual-token economy, and social finance, making trading faster, fairer, smarter, and more transparent.

Through innovative product design and intelligent liquidity systems, MEXQuick is building a safe, efficient, and sustainable financial ecosystem for users worldwide.

***

## Our Mission & Vision

### Mission

To make it easy for anyone to join the derivatives market, share real liquidity profits, and promote smart and inclusive finance.

### Vision

To become the world’s leading short-term derivatives and social finance platform, using AI technology to improve efficiency and fairness in the market.

### Core Goal

To build a transparent, trusted, and sustainable global financial ecosystem through innovation and global expansion.
