![Supporting Strategy Image](<.gitbook/assets/image (54)>)

[Direct link to heading](supporting-strategy.md#id-1.-liquidity-management)

### Strategic Summary 📈

[Direct link to strategic summary](supporting-strategy.md#strategic-summary)

The development of **MQT** follows the path of:

“Starting from DEX → Expanding to Medium-Sized CEX → Recognition by Leading CEX → Endogenous Parallel Development in the Ecosystem.”

This structured roadmap ensures both **short-term liquidity** and **long-term value support**, enabling MQT to:

* Gain **recognition in the trading market**, and
* Form **rigid demand in ecological applications**.

✨ _In essence, MQT evolves through a balanced model — liquidity-first, compliance-secured, and ecosystem-driven — ensuring sustainable global growth._
