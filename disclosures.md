## ⚖️ Disclosure and Risk Policy

MEXQuick is committed to ensuring that users fully understand the **risks** and **regulations** associated with digital asset trading and platform operations. All disclosures are made to promote **user awareness**, **data transparency**, and **responsible participation** within the ecosystem.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Risk Disclosure ⚠️

#### 📉 Market Risk

Digital asset prices can fluctuate **sharply and unpredictably**, which may result in the **total loss of principal** for investors.

#### 💧 Liquidity Risk

In extreme market conditions or with specific products, there may be **insufficient liquidity**, **delayed transactions**, or **increased slippage**.

#### ⚡ Leverage and Derivatives Risk

Products such as **Ticket Contracts**, **Event Contracts**, and **Rhythm Contracts** involve **high leverage** and **short cycles**, which carry significant potential for **rapid losses**.

#### 🛠️ System and Technical Risk

Network interruptions, server failures, third-party service outages, or **hacker attacks** could impact **order execution** or **asset security**.

#### ⚖️ Policy and Legal Risk

Users are solely responsible for ensuring that their **trading activities** comply with all **applicable laws and regulations** in their jurisdiction.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Pricing and Market Data Sources 📊

* The **price index** aggregates spot and derivative market data from **multiple leading exchanges** (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Kraken, etc.).
* Prices are refreshed every **500 milliseconds** for accuracy and fairness.
* The system automatically **filters out outlier data** that deviates ±5% from the median value.
* In case of data feed interruption, a **backup pricing mechanism** is activated to maintain continuity.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Conflicts of Interest and Market Making Disclosure 🔗

* MEXQuick may cooperate with **independent market makers** or external liquidity providers to ensure sufficient **market depth**.
* The platform’s objective is **not to profit from user losses**, and it does **not act as a counterparty** to user transactions unless explicitly disclosed.
* If **proprietary trading** or **counterparty arrangements** are involved, they will be **publicly disclosed** in accordance with regulatory requirements.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Disclosure of Fees and Charges 💰

* All **transaction**, **withdrawal**, **network mining**, and related fees are published on the platform’s **official Fee Page** and within official announcements.
* Temporary **event-based discounts** or promotions will automatically **revert to standard rates** once the campaign period ends.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

> ⚖️ _MEXQuick upholds transparency, fair trading, and compliance — empowering users to participate with full awareness of all risks and disclosures._
