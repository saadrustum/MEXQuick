MEXQuick's dual-currency economic model forms a positive cycle of "capital inflow → income creation → distribution incentives → token consumption → scarcity appreciation" through the coordinated operation of MUSD (stable anchor) and MQT (value capture).

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Inflow: User Funds Enter

MUSD Flow: USDT Conversion, Staking, and Risk Isolation

* Users use USDT to exchange for MUSD.
* MUSD is used as a pledge certificate in the market-making pool and enters the AI market-making system.
* The platform ensures fund isolation: USDT is used in the trading layer, and MUSD is used in the market-making layer, so risks are controllable.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Output: Platform Generates Revenue

Revenue Model: Multiple Streams Aggregated into Profit Pool

* Transaction Fees: A fixed 10% for event contracts and rhythm contracts.
* Entrance Fee Deposits: A portion of the fixed entrance fee for ticket contracts is deposited.
* Spread Capture: AI market makers lock in the bid-ask spread through pending orders.
* Cross-Market Arbitrage: Hedge and arbitrage in external markets such as Binance, OKX, and Superp.
* All profits are aggregated into a profit pool.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Distribution: 70/20/10 Mechanism

User rewards, risk buffer, deflation through buyback and burn

* 70% → Users: Market makers receive a share of the profits, which are uniformly distributed in MQT.
* 20% → Risk Control Fund: Used as a risk buffer in extreme market conditions to ensure stability.
* 10% → Buyback and Destruction: The platform regularly uses the proceeds to buy back MQT and destroy it, creating deflation.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Circulation: MQT Usage and Consumption

MQT empowers users with discounts, fast swaps, asset trading, and governance.

* Fee deduction: Users who pay transaction fees with MQT can enjoy a 50% discount.
* Flash exchange mechanism: MQT → USDT one-way flash exchange, 10% fee (5% destruction + 5% risk control fund).
* UID market: MQT can be used for P2P transactions, NFT, and equity card circulation.
* Guild and governance: Holding MQT means having the right to participate in and govern the ecosystem.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Value End: Forming a Closed Loop

* Users deposit USDT → convert to MUSD → market making.
* The platform generates real income → distributes it in MQT.
* Users obtain MQT → can reinvest, deduct transaction fees, or trade on the UID market.
* MQT is continuously consumed and partially destroyed during use, reducing the circulation supply.
* Scarcity + demand for use → drives up the value of MQT.
* Increased MQT value → enhances users' willingness to hold and participate → attracts more funds into the MUSD market making pool.

This creates a long-term, sustainable token economic cycle:

* MUSD ensures stable inflows and secure settlements.
* MQT drives value growth and ecosystem expansion.

MEXQuick's dual-currency circulation logic ensures a dynamic balance between user returns, platform stability, and token value, ensuring the ecosystem's long-term vitality of self-growth and self-circulation.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
