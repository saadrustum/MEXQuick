# Key Features

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (13)>)

Low Cost · High Leverage · Zero Liquidation · Fair Pricing

* Low-Cost, High-Value Exposure\
  Gain significant exposure to price fluctuations with a fixed entry fee, eliminating the need for large capital deposits.
* Dynamic High Leverage\
  Leverage is low at the beginning of a cycle, automatically increasing to thousands of times as maturity approaches, offering significant profit potential for short-term trading.
* No Margin Call, Capped Risk\
  Maximum loss is fixed at the entry fee, with no forced liquidations or margin calls.
* Fair and Transparent Pricing\
  Based on a multi-source aggregated index price, updated every 500ms, eliminating outliers and preventing price manipulation.
* Slippage-Free Execution\
  Using an aggregated index price, execution doesn't rely on order book depth, removing slippage caused by insufficient liquidity.
* Transparent Fee Policy\
  Transaction fees are temporarily waived, with a fixed 10% profit charge at settlement. Rules are clear and transparent.
