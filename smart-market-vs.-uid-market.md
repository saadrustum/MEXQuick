# Smart Market vs. UID Market

![Smart Market vs UID Market](<.gitbook/assets/image (18)>)

MEXQuick has designed a dual-market architecture to balance financial liquidity and social growth:

* Smart Market: ensures stablecoin liquidity while forming a closed loop of MQT value;
* UID Market: promotes social transactions and community fission, and builds a new social financial experience.

### Comparison Table

| Dimensions    |                                                                                                                                                Smart Market | UID Market (P2P Social Market)                                                                                                                                                           |
| ------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Position      |                                                              One-click flash exchange center ensures stable anchoring of MUSD and provides MQT value export | Socialized secondary market based on UID identity                                                                                                                                        |
| Core Features |                                                                     <p>- MUSD ↔ USDT two-way swap<br>- MQT → USDT one-way flash swap (10% handling fee)</p> | <p>- UID peer-to-peer matching<br>- UID vs. UID PK, guild gameplay</p>                                                                                                                   |
| Fee mechanism |                                                                         <p>- MUSD ↔ USDT: fixed at 1 USDT<br>- MQT → USDT: 10% (5% burn + 5% fund pool)</p> | <p>- Asset transfer: 5% (70% platform + 30% risk control fund)<br>- NFT/equity card: fixed rate, partial destruction of MQT<br>- UID battle: 10% battle fee, part of the reward pool</p> |
| Advantages    | <p>- Fast: One-click flash exchange, transaction in seconds<br>- Stable: MUSD maintains a 1:1 anchor<br>- Closed value loop: MQT fees support deflation</p> | <p>- Simple and intuitive: no reliance on order books<br>- Decentralized: the platform only does clearing<br>- Socially driven: trading is interaction</p>                               |
| Future Plans  |                                                  <p>- Cross-chain flash exchange, multi-chain assets<br>- Open API interface<br>- Decentralized custody</p> | <p>- NFT battles, guild system<br>- UID secondary market<br>- RWA, GameFi expansion</p>                                                                                                  |

### Summary Value

The Smart Market and UID Market complement each other:

* One focuses on financial liquidity, ensuring capital stability and MQT value circulation;
* The other is more socially driven, enhancing user stickiness and community fission.

This dual-market architecture enables MEXQuick to simultaneously achieve efficient capital circulation and carry the social value of identity, jointly building the platform's liquidity center and social finance foundation.
