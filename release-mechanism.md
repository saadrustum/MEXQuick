# Release Mechanism

![Total Quantity and Issuance Policy](<.gitbook/assets/image (61)>)

## Community Incentives and Rewards

* Released daily based on "market making output + event rewards".
* Tied to user staking and community contributions to ensure authentic and effective release.
* Estimated cycle: 5–10 years.

## Ecosystem Development and Fund

* Released in phases as the ecosystem progresses.
* Used for risk management supplements, NFT, guild, RWA, and other expansion.
* Focused on long-term development, avoiding short-term secondary market entry.

## Liquidity and Market Expansion

* Released gradually as the token launch progresses.
* Partial long-term lockup as a price stabilization fund to prevent drastic fluctuations.

## Team and Advisors

* Lockup period: 12 months.
* Unlock period: After lockup expires, funds will be linearly released monthly/quarterly over 24–36 months.
* Purpose: Ensure the team's long-term commitment to platform development.

## Early Investors and Strategic Partners

* Lockup period: 6–12 months.
* Unlock period: After lockup expires, funds will be linearly released over 12–24 months.
* Purpose: Ensure early support while avoiding short-term selling pressure.

## Reserve Fund (Long-term Reserve)

* 5% of the total supply.
* Used for risk control and ecosystem expansion.
* Only used in extreme market conditions or major ecosystem developments; not normally put into circulation.
