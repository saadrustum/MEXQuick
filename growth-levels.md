# Growth Levels

#### Upgrade Conditions 🔼

The MEXQuick Market-Making Tier System combines direct referral requirements with personal contribution metrics to ensure fair growth and reward participation. Users advance through levels by meeting both structural (referral) and performance-based (contribution) criteria.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Direct Referrals (Hard Requirement) 👥

Each tier requires a minimum number of direct referral users.

Valid Users are defined as individuals who meet at least one of the following conditions:

* Staking ≥ 100 MUSD, or
* Personal daily trading volume ≥ 100 USDT

Direct referrals validate community expansion and ensure active participation within the ecosystem.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Personal Contribution (Choose One) 💰

Users can fulfill their upgrade requirement through either staking or daily trading activity — whichever is greater.

Staking Amount

* Refers to the total MUSD a user locks in the market-making pool to supply liquidity.
* The system automatically generates daily returns, while the staked principal remains redeemable according to platform rules.

Daily Trading Volume

* Refers to the total amount (in USDT) of all buys and sells executed by the user, regardless of profit or loss.
* When the cumulative trading volume meets the tier’s requirement, the user qualifies for an upgrade.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

⚖️ Level Progression Formula

Level Progression = Direct Referrals (Valid Users) + Personal Contribution (Staking or Trading Volume)

This dual-condition mechanism ensures that each advancement reflects both network growth and individual engagement, maintaining balance between community expansion and user participation.

#### 💎 Reward Mechanism

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Promotion Rewards

Each time a user successfully upgrades, they will immediately receive a corresponding USDT reward.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Invitation Rewards

When a direct referral friend completes an upgrade, the inviter also receives a reward of the same amount.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The MEXQuick Market-Making Tier System ensures a balanced and merit-based growth model, combining both community expansion and individual activity. By linking staking, trading, and referrals, it creates a self-sustaining ecosystem that rewards dedication, participation, and contribution.
