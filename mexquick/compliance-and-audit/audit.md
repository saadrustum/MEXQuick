🛡️ **MEXQuick Security & Transparency Policy**

MEXQuick upholds **security and transparency** as its core operating principles. A multi-layered audit, verification, and improvement framework has been established to ensure user trust, platform integrity, and long-term reliability.

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Platform Security Audit 🔍

* Independent third-party security agencies are regularly commissioned to conduct **penetration tests, system security assessments, and source code reviews**.
* The platform’s **transaction systems, protection mechanisms, and risk control strategies** are routinely examined to ensure operational effectiveness and resilience.

[Read the platform security audit section](audit.md#id-1.-platform-security-audit)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Proof of Reserves 📊

* MEXQuick **regularly publishes audit results** verifying that user asset reserves maintain a **1:1 ratio** with platform custody holdings.
* Fund safety is reinforced through **hot–cold wallet segregation**, **multi-signature authorization**, and **multi-level approval protocols** for sensitive operations.

[Read the proof of reserves section](audit.md#id-2.-proof-of-reserves)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Smart Contract Audit (To Be Launched After Listing) ⚙️

* Prior to deploying platform tokens or related on-chain functionalities, **globally recognized auditors** such as _CertiK_ and _SlowMist_ will conduct **smart contract security audits**.
* The audits will focus on **vulnerability scanning, permission structure review, and logic verification**, followed by ongoing evaluations when updates are made.

[Read the smart contract audit section](audit.md#id-3.-smart-contract-audit-to-be-launched-after-listing)
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Transparency & Continuous Improvement 💡

* All **audit reports and reserve certificates** will be disclosed via the official website and public announcement channels.
* A **bug bounty program** encourages responsible disclosure by ethical hackers and security researchers.
* MEXQuick remains committed to **continuous optimization** of its security and compliance systems, aligning with evolving regulations and global standards.

[Read the transparency & improvement section](audit.md#id-4.-transparency-and-continuous-improvement)
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
