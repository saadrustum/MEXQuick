# Regional Partner

![](<../../.gitbook/assets/image (23)>)

Regional Partners are MEXQuick's core partners in global expansion. Partners are independently responsible for market development and user maintenance in their respective regions, and they enjoy additional regional dividends on top of the regular three-level rebates.

## Rights and interests

* Equity Projects
* Regional promotion rights\
  Independently responsible for market development and user maintenance in the region
* Revenue Sharing\
  Additional commissions (USDT) on the regional team's trading volume + 1%–3% of market making revenue (MQT)
* Event Support\
  Priority in leading local market activities and enjoying official publicity and material support
* Official endorsement\
  Obtain MEXQuick official certification and training, and the status is valid for a long time

## Joining conditions

* Possess a mature local trading community or promotion channel
* The team must have ≥ 100 direct referrals and a cumulative team stake of ≥ 50,000 MUSD
* Possess practical market development and operational capabilities
* Submit an application through the official website or official customer service, and sign a cooperation agreement after approval

## Examples

Assume you become an agent in a certain region, where the local team's daily trading volume is 5,000,000 USDT and their daily market-making revenue is 200,000 MQT (converted to USDT). The platform will grant you an additional 2% regional commission:

* Trading volume commission: 5,000,000 × 2% = 100,000 USDT/day
* Market-making commission: 200,000 × 2% = 4,000 MQT/day

This totals 100,000 USDT + 4,000 MQT/day in additional revenue, which is added to your existing three-tier rebate.
