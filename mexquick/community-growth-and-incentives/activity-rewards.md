## Activity Rewards

MEXQuick encourages active participation through multiple reward programs designed to promote consistency, teamwork, and engagement.

### Continuous Trading Rewards 💹

| Activity       |                                                 Condition |   Reward | Rules                             |
| -------------- | --------------------------------------------------------: | -------: | --------------------------------- |
| 7-Day Mission  |    Daily trading volume ≥ 500 USDT for 7 consecutive days |  50 USDT | Missing a day resets the mission. |
| 30-Day Mission | Daily trading volume ≥ 1,000 USDT for 30 consecutive days | 500 USDT | Missing a day resets the mission. |

{% hint style="info" %}
Consistency pays — stay active to earn progressive rewards.
{% endhint %}

### Mystery Box Event 🎁

**How to Obtain**

* Exchange 49 USDT or equivalent MQT for one Mystery Box
* Invite friends who deposit ≥ 50 USDT to earn an additional box

**Prizes**

* Cash rewards (USDT)
* Platform bonuses (unlockable after trading)
* MQT tokens (core platform rewards)
* Double reward multipliers
* Physical prizes (iPhone, MacBook, Apple Watch, etc.)

**Rules**

* Each box reveals one random prize
* Physical prizes distributed after official notice
* Platform bonuses require trading unlock
* MQT and cash rewards credited instantly

{% hint style="info" %}
Every box is a chance to win — from instant token bonuses to premium prizes.
{% endhint %}
