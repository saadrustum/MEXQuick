# Total Quantity and Issuance Policy

![Total Quantity and Issuance Policy](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (35)>)

* Total Supply: The total supply of MQT is fixed at 10 billion, minted once and never increased.
* Issuance Policy: Adopting a "distribute first, release later" model, all tokens are reserved according to the allocation ratio and released according to the established unlocking curve to ensure market transparency and long-term stability.
