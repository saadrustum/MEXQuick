# MUSD × MQT Collaboration and Process (Operational Aspects)

![](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (16)>)

* Users hold USDT.
* In the Smart Market, USDT ↔ MUSD are exchanged two-way with low fee; MUSD → USDT has a fixed fee of 1 USDT.
* Users pledge MUSD to enter the market-making pool to participate in market making.
* The platform issues market-making commissions / agent commissions / incentives in MQT according to the rules.

MQT usage options:

* Used to offset commissions.
* Traded with others in the UID Market (P2P).
* Swapped MQT → USDT in the Smart Market (10% commission: 5% burn + 5% fund pool).
* Exercised for rights and voting in scenarios such as NFTs, guilds, and governance.

The continuous operation of burns and fund pools enables MQT to capture value in the use → burn → buyback cycle.

USDT is responsible for settlement at the transaction layer, MUSD is responsible for staking certificates at the market-making layer, and MQT is responsible for income and rights — the responsibilities of the three are clear and they do not interfere with each other.
