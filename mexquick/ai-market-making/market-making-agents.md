# Market-Making Agents

MEXQuick believes that a true financial ecosystem is not driven by isolated actions but by a connected community network. To support this vision, MEXQuick has introduced a three-tier agent profit-sharing mechanism — enabling users to evolve from individual market makers into community partners. By helping the platform grow, participants share in the long-term success of the expanding ecosystem.

[**Profit-Sharing Rules**](market-making-agents.md#profit-sharing-rules)

Agents earn a percentage of the market-making profits generated by their referral network. All profits are settled in MQT and distributed in real time under clear, transparent rules.

| Level       | Description             | Commission Rate | Source                                                               |
| ----------- | ----------------------- | --------------: | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Level 1** | Direct referral users   |              2% | Market-making profits of directly referred users                     |
| **Level 2** | Indirect referral users |              1% | Market-making profits of users referred by your direct referrals     |
| **Level 3** | Third-level users       |              1% | Market-making profits of users referred by your second-level members |

***

[**Community Partner Path**](market-making-agents.md#community-partner-path)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### From Individual to Community Builder

Your journey begins as an individual participant. Once you invite your first user, you become a community builder — earning not only from your own staking but also from the performance of your directly referred users.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Expanding Your Network

As your direct referrals invite more members, your community naturally grows to include second and third levels. Your profit-sharing expands layer by layer, transforming your income source from an individual effort into a network-driven model.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Becoming a Community Partner

When your team grows to dozens or even hundreds of members, your role evolves into that of a true community partner. At this stage, your profit share can exceed your personal market-making earnings. The more active your community, the more consistent and sustainable your long-term returns become.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

At MEXQuick, agents are more than inviters — they are partners in growth. Each invitation becomes a node that contributes to your long-term earnings, while each stage of community expansion strengthens your profit network. The result is a sustainable, transparent, and rewarding ecosystem where every participant benefits from shared success.
