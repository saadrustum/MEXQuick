June 2025 | Rhythm Contract & Multi-Source Aggregate Pricing System Launched ⚡

![Rhythm Contracts](<../.gitbook/assets/image (7)>)

Officially released Rhythm Contracts and a multi-source aggregated pricing system.

Core Features:

* Unified opening and settlement: all users receive the same price in the same round
* Real-time price capture from Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Kraken, etc., updated every 500ms
* Elimination of latency advantages, ensuring outcomes depend on judgment, not hand speed

October 2025 | AI Market-Making System & TOKEN2049 Singapore 🤖

![AI Market-Making](<../.gitbook/assets/image (11)>)

AI Market-Making System Open:

* Users pledge MUSD to enter the market-making pool
* System prioritizes internal matching of long and short orders
* Unoffset net exposure is hedged externally by the AI market-making pool
* Profits are settled in MQT and distributed daily

This marks the first time ordinary users can provide liquidity like institutions.

TOKEN2049 Singapore Cryptocurrency Summit:

* In-depth exchanges with global investors, industry media, and potential partners
* Showcased MEXQuick’s product ecosystem and token economic model
