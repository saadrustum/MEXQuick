# Risk Management

![](../../../.gitbook/assets/image)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Maximum Loss Cap

The traditional liquidation mechanism has been eliminated for ticket contracts, with the maximum loss now fixed at the entry fee.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fair and Transparent Pricing

Utilizing the MEXQuick multi-source aggregated pricing system, with 500ms updates, we eliminate outliers and ensure fairness and transparency.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Non-Gambling Matching

The platform does not wager against users and only acts as a liquidator. Profits and losses are settled through user-to-user matching or through a neutral liquidity pool.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Market Maker and Institutional Liquidity Support

Professional market makers and institutional partners provide liquidity, ensuring deep and smooth trading.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Position Size Limits

Limits are set for individual users and the platform as a whole to prevent concentrated risk.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Extreme Market Protection

Liquidity pool hedging, suspension of abnormal fluctuations, and automatic position reduction (ADL).
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Continuous Expansion and Partnership

Integrate with more international liquidity institutions and market makers to diversify risks.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
