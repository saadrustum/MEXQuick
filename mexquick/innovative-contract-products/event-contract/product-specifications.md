# Product Specifications

![Product Parameters](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (4)>)

### Parameter Category

* **Illustrate**
* **Underlying Assets**\
  Currently supports BTC/USDT, and will expand to ETH/USDT and other mainstream and high-volatility assets in the future
* **Forecast period**\
  Fixed cycle: 5 minute / 10 minutes / 30 minutes
* **Trading direction**\
  Bullish (↑) / Bearish (↓)
* **Investment amount**\
  User-defined (i.e. maximum possible loss amount)
* **Rate of Return**\
  Fixed 90% — if the prediction is correct, you will receive the principal + principal × 90%
* **Handling Fees**\
  Fixed 10%; 50% discount available when using MQT
* **Settlement Method**\
  Automatic settlement upon maturity — no need to close positions manually
* **Price Source**\
  Aggregate prices from multiple sources (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Kraken, etc.), update every 500ms, and remove outliers
* **Trading Hours**\
  7×24 hours continuous participation
* **Risk Control Mechanism**\
  Single user holding limit + platform total holding limit to prevent extreme market concentration risks
