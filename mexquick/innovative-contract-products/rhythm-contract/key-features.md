# Key Features

![Key Features](<../../../.gitbook/assets/image (13)>)

* Fair, unified pricing mechanism — All users experience the same opening and settlement prices, completely eliminating latency advantages.
* Fixed round cadence — Each round includes a green countdown (order window) and a red countdown (holding period), ensuring a clear trading rhythm.
* Fixed risk, transparent reward — The investment amount represents the maximum loss, and the rate of return is fixed at 90%.
* Strategy-focused — Success or failure depends on market analysis, not order execution speed, making it ideal for strategic traders.
* 24/7 trading — Continuous trading is available 24/7, allowing you to participate at any time.
* Discounted handling fee mechanism — The default handling fee is 10%, and paying with MQT offers a 50% discount.
