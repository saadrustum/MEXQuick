## Universal Fair Pricing: Everyone Enters at the Same Price

Rhythm Contract uses a multi-source aggregated pricing system (Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Kraken, etc.), updated every 500 milliseconds, eliminating abnormal data that deviates from the median price by ±5%, ensuring that all users receive the same entry and settlement prices, and ensuring fairness and transparency.
