# Risk Management

![](.gitbook/assets/image)

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Fixed risk, clear upper limit

The investment amount represents the maximum possible loss.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Fair and transparent pricing mechanism

Aggregated market data from multiple sources, updated every 500ms, with outliers eliminated.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Non-betting settlement model

The platform does not bet against users; profits and losses are settled through user-to-user matching or a neutral pool.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Position limit management

Position limits for individual users and for the entire platform prevent concentrated risk in extreme market conditions.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Extreme market protection

New positions can be suspended in the event of price anomalies, and hedging can be achieved through liquidity pools to ensure settlement stability.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}
