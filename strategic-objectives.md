# Strategic Objectives

![Strategic Objectives](<.gitbook/assets/image (32)>)

The listing and development of MQT is not just about listing on an exchange, but rather about supporting the value of the platform's overall strategy:

* **Liquidity Goal**: Ensure users can trade freely at any time, improving depth and stability.
* **Ecosystem Goal**: Ensure MQT has real use cases in platform products, rather than being a single speculative tool.
* **Compliance Goal**: Gradually align with the regulatory requirements of major jurisdictions around the world to pave the way for long-term operations.
* **Brand Goal**: Leverage the listing event to expand market influence and attract agents, institutions, and new users.
