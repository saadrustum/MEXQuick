# Product Positioning

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (24)>)

Designed for Strategic Traders and Fair Competition Enthusiasts

Rhythm contracts are suitable for users who value fairness and prefer a consistent trading rhythm. Their fixed-round mechanism eliminates differences in order speed, making them more suitable for traders who rely on analysis and strategy. They are also well-suited for social play and group prediction scenarios.
