### Positioning

**Revenue Distribution** All rewards — including market-making commissions, agent commissions, and activity incentives — are distributed in **MQT**, ensuring a unified and transparent value flow.

**Value Tools** MQT serves as a multifunctional asset for **fee deductions**, **ecosystem governance**, and **equity certification**. Deflation and long-term value accumulation are achieved through **regular buybacks and token burns**.

**Total Supply and Policy**

* **Total Supply:** 10 billion MQT (fixed cap, no additional issuance)
* **Monetary Policy Principle:** _Output → Consumption → Deflation_ — a structure to ensure long-term scarcity and sustainable value appreciation.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Acquisition Methods

Only three legitimate ways exist to acquire MQT — each tied to real contributions to the ecosystem:

* **Market Making:** Stake **MUSD** in the market-making pool to generate MQT income according to platform rules.
* **Market-Making Agents:** Earn **commissions** through direct referrals and team contributions, with rewards settled in MQT.
* **UID Market (P2P):** Buy MQT **peer-to-peer** with USDT inside the platform, allowing free market pricing.

{% hint style="info" %}
No unconditional airdrops or random external releases. Every MQT originates from measurable participation within the system.
{% endhint %}
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Use and Consumption

**Fee Deduction**

* Standard contract fee: **10%**
* **50% discount** when fees are paid using MQT (subject to official adjustments)

**Smart Market Flash Swap**

* Supports **one-way flash swaps** from **MQT → USDT**
* **10% fee allocation:**
  * 🔥 **5%** used to repurchase and burn MQT (deflation & scarcity)
  * 💰 **5%** injected into the platform fund pool (risk management + community incentives)

Reverse swaps (**USDT → MQT**) are **not supported**, preventing speculation and market shocks.

**Ecosystem Benefits** MQT functions as a **participation and governance token**, used across ecosystem features such as:

* UID Market
* Guild and Community Systems
* NFT Battles and Equity Modules
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Value-Driven Structure

**Endogenous Demand** Generated by market-making commissions, agent earnings, and event incentives — promoting organic MQT holding.

**Functional Demand** Arises from fee discounts, P2P transfers, and ecosystem interactions like NFTs and guild activities.

**Deflationary Mechanism** Continuous burning from flash swaps and transaction fees, alongside regular buybacks, ensures declining circulation and rising scarcity.

**Governance Binding** Gradual rollout of governance rights — enabling MQT holders to participate in decision-making, aligning long-term holding with platform growth.
{% endstep %}
