# Product Vision

![Product Vision](<.gitbook/assets/image (38)>)

MEXQuick aims to establish a differentiated competitive advantage in the high-leverage derivatives market through ticket contracts, enabling more traders to participate in the market with clear and manageable risk while enjoying the potential for high returns. This design transforms high-risk, high-leverage trading into a new trading model with low barriers to entry, a high experience, and manageable costs. This is expected to attract a large number of novice users to the derivatives market while improving the capital utilization efficiency of experienced traders.
