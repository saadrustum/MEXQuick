# Product Specifications

![Product Parameters](<.gitbook/assets/image (4)>)

illustrate

* Underlying Assets\
  Currently supports BTC/USDT, and will expand to ETH/USDT and other mainstream and high-volatility assets in the future
* Transaction cycle\
  Fixed rounds: order window (e.g. 2 minutes) + holding period (1–3 minutes)
* Trading direction\
  Bullish (↑) / Bearish (↓)
* Investment amount\
  User-defined (i.e. maximum loss amount)
* Rate of Return\
  Fixed 90% — if the prediction is correct, you will get the principal + principal × 90%
* Handling Fees\
  Fixed 10%; 50% discount for payment with MQT
* Settlement Method\
  Unified settlement: unified opening of positions at the end of the window period and unified settlement at the end of the cycle
* Price Source\
  Aggregate prices from multiple sources, update within 500ms, eliminate outliers, and ensure fair prices.
* Trading Hours\
  7×24 hours continuous operation
* Risk Control Mechanism\
  Single user position limit + platform total position limit to prevent extreme market concentration risks
