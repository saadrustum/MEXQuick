## Ticket Contracts — Overview

Up to 10,000X Leverage · Exit Anytime in 10 Minutes

Ticket Contracts are an innovative, short-term, high-leverage product launched by **MEXQuick** in the derivatives trading field. They provide traders with a more **efficient, flexible, and controllable** way to participate in the market through a **fixed-cost, dynamic leverage, and no-margin-explosion** model.

### The problem with traditional high-leverage trading

* Requires substantial **margin deposits**.
* Carries constant **risk of liquidation** — a brief market reversal can wipe out the principal within seconds.
* While offering **high-yield potential**, it brings **significant risks and psychological stress**, often deterring short-term or novice traders.

### The Ticket Contract solution

* **No Margin Required:** Replaces the traditional margin system with a **one-time fixed entry fee**.
* **Limited Loss:** The **maximum loss equals the entry fee**, fully eliminating liquidation risk.
* **Short-Term Cycle:** Each contract lasts **10 minutes**, allowing users to react quickly and re-enter trades easily.

### Dynamic leverage mechanism

* Leverage **increases naturally** as the contract nears expiration.
* Can reach **thousands of times** leverage near the end of the cycle.
* Enables traders to **maximize yield flexibility** and capture short-term volatility efficiently.

### Reliable pricing system

* Powered by **MEXQuick’s multi-source aggregated pricing**, updated every **500ms**.
* Ensures **fair and consistent** entry and settlement prices for all users.
* **Prevents slippage** and **eliminates price manipulation**.

### Ideal for

* **Short-term traders** seeking quick, high-value returns at low cost.
* **Speculators** targeting volatile or trending cryptocurrencies.
* **Strategic users** looking for short-term **hedging opportunities**.

In essence, Ticket Contracts balance high-yield potential with controllable risk — making them a smart choice for traders who value speed, flexibility, and protection from liquidation.
