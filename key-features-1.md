# Key Features

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (13)>)

* Simple rules, easy to use — Simply predict the price direction (up or down) at expiration, no complex derivatives knowledge required.
* Short, fast cycle — Three fixed cycles are available: 5 minute, 10 minutes, and 30 minutes, providing rapid feedback.
* Fixed risk, transparent return — The investment amount represents the maximum loss, and the rate of return is fixed at 90%.
* Fair and transparent multi-source pricing — Aggregates prices from multiple exchanges, updates every 500ms, and eliminates outliers.
* 24/7 trading — Participate in market forecasts 24/7.
* Fee discounts — The default fee is 10%, and payment with MQT offers a 50% discount.
