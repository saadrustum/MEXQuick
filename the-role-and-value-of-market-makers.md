# The Role and Value of Market Makers

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (49)>)

Market makers are specialized market participants responsible for providing liquidity and maintaining price stability. Unlike traders who simply bet on market direction, market makers' core strategy is to achieve relatively stable returns and ensure smooth market operation through continuous order placement and risk management.

### Market Maker’s Revenue Model

* Spread capture: Market makers place buy and sell orders simultaneously, profiting from the difference between the buy and sell prices.\
  Example: In the BTC/USDT market, if the buy price is 50,000 and the sell price is 50,100, each trade locks in a 100 USDT spread.
* Fee rebates: On traditional exchanges, market makers often enjoy lower or even negative fee rates. On MEXQuick, market makers can also share in the fee income generated by user transactions.
* Volatility capture: Uses AI algorithms and hedging strategies to enable market makers to repeatedly capture price differences during price fluctuations, thereby obtaining additional premiums.

### The market role of market makers

* Providing market depth – preventing extreme price fluctuations caused by a single large order.
* Improving liquidity – allowing users to place orders at any time and receive a response from their counterparties.
* Ensuring fairness – reducing the risk of gambling or manipulation through unified quotes and hedging mechanisms.
* Risk neutralization – leveraging external markets (Binance, OKX, Superp) for hedging to maintain overall market balance.

### Market Making Mechanism in MEXQuick

In short-term leveraged products like event contracts, rhythm contracts, and ticket contracts, user orders often create an imbalance between long and short positions. MEXQuick's AI market-making system operates through the following mechanisms:

* Internal matching: first, long and short orders are offset against each other.
* External hedging: the unoffset net difference is covered by the AI market-making pool, while a reverse position is established in the external market.
* Revenue composition: ticket fees, transaction fees, and volatility arbitrage are combined to generate relatively stable returns.

MEXQuick not only ensures the liquidity and fairness of the derivatives market, but also allows ordinary users to participate in market making by staking MUSD and enjoy liquidity income opportunities close to institutional level.
