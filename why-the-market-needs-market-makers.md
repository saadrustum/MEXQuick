# Why the Market Needs Market Makers

![](<.gitbook/assets/image (73)>)

The fundamental functions of financial markets are price discovery and liquidity provision. Without sustained liquidity support, market mechanisms tend to develop structural imbalances, which can be observed in several key areas:

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Liquidity Shortage

When the **order book depth** is insufficient, even a single large order can trigger significant price deviations, resulting in severe **slippage**. In derivatives markets, such imbalances directly undermine **trading fairness** and **price predictability**.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Spread Widening

Without consistent **market-making activity**, the **bid-ask gap** widens abnormally. This forces users to trade at **unfavorable prices**, raising **transaction costs** and reducing overall market efficiency.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Price Manipulation Risk

In **low-liquidity environments**, small amounts of capital can disproportionately affect prices. This increases the **risk of manipulation** and reduces market transparency — a critical concern for **short-term derivatives** and other fast-moving instruments.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Volatility Amplification

A lack of liquidity buffers causes markets to **overreact** to short-term sentiment or external shocks. Minor fluctuations can quickly escalate into **sharp market movements**, heightening **systemic risk** and investor uncertainty.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Retail Disadvantage

In thinly traded markets, prices are often **dominated by large funds**, leaving retail participants exposed to **excessive slippage** and **unequal trading conditions**. This imbalance erodes **user confidence** and weakens **long-term participation**.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

The presence of **market makers** is essential for maintaining **market efficiency, fairness, and stability**. By continuously providing liquidity, placing orders, and managing risk, market makers:

* Reduce **trading friction**
* Curb **abnormal volatility**
* Support a **balanced and transparent** trading environment

Without them, markets would struggle to sustain **effective price discovery**, discouraging long-term capital inflows and hindering healthy market development.
