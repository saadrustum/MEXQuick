## Dimensions comparison

| Dimension                      |                                                  Ticket Contract |                                      Perpetual Contracts / Futures |
| ------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------------------------------: | -----------------------------------------------------------------: |
| Risk                           |                               Maximum loss is fixed at entry fee | With market fluctuations, the position may be liquidated instantly |
| Leverage                       |                                 Dynamic Adjustment (100x and up) |                                       Fixed or manually adjustable |
| Margin                         |                                          No margin call required |                        A margin is required and must be maintained |
| Trading psychological pressure |                                      Low (no forced liquidation) |        High (real-time monitoring of margin call line is required) |
| Cycle                          |                                             Fixed for 10 minutes |                                                     No fixed cycle |
| Applicable people              | Short-term speculation, hot currency trading, short-term hedging |                                Professional high-frequency traders |

The launch of the ticket contract means that MEXQuick has added a track specifically designed for extreme leverage + short-cycle strategies to its product matrix, forming a triple complement with the platform's existing rhythm contracts and event contracts to meet the trading needs of different types of users.
