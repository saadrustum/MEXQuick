{% hint style="warning" %}
Digital asset trading is highly volatile, and prices may fluctuate dramatically in a short period of time. You may face the risk of losing your entire investment capital.

Highly leveraged products (such as ticket contracts, event contracts, and rhythm contracts) may magnify gains or losses in a very short period of time and are not suitable for all investors.

Market conditions are affected by many factors, including but not limited to policy changes, market sentiment, and the macroeconomic environment.
{% endhint %}
