## Synchronized Trading: Compete on Strategy, Not Speed

**Rhythm Contracts** are an innovative short-term predictive derivatives trading model developed by **MEXQuick**. By introducing fixed trading cycles with synchronized position opening and settlement, this model eliminates unfairness caused by order speed and network latency. As a result, traders can focus on **strategy and market judgment** rather than reaction time.

### Key Features

{% stepper %}
{% step %}
### Unified Opening Time

All participants submit orders within a preset order window (e.g., 2 minutes). Once the window closes, all positions are opened simultaneously.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Unified Entry Price

Every participant in the same round opens a position at **the same price**, ensuring full pricing fairness.
{% endstep %}

{% step %}
### Unified Settlement Time and Price

After a fixed holding period (e.g., 1–3 minutes), all positions are settled at **the same price**, and profits or losses are calculated accordingly.
{% endstep %}
{% endstepper %}

### Advantages

This model completely removes the traditional advantage of faster order placement. In **Rhythm Contracts**, every trader competes on **analytical ability and market prediction**, not speed or latency. It provides a **level playing field** for short-term traders, emphasizing fairness, strategic decision-making, and predictive accuracy.
