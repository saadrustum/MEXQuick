# Fairness and Transparency

![Fairness & Transparency](<.gitbook/assets/image (51)>)

Event contracts use multi-source aggregated market data as the pricing basis (including Binance, Coinbase, OKX, Kraken, etc.), updated every 500 milliseconds. Abnormal data that deviates from the median price by ±5% is eliminated to ensure the entry price and settlement price are fair, transparent, and resistant to manipulation.

{% hint style="info" %}
Pricing sources are aggregated across multiple exchanges and refreshed every 500 ms. Data points deviating more than ±5% from the median are filtered out.
{% endhint %}
