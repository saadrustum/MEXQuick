# Product Positioning

![Product Positioning](<.gitbook/assets/image (24)>)

#### Designed for Fast-Paced Traders and Market Speculators

With simple rules, a fast pace, and fixed risk, event contracts are particularly suitable for fast-paced traders, speculators seeking high returns with small amounts of capital, and traders who want to quickly bet on the direction of a hot market. The product supports 24/7 trading, allowing users to seize market opportunities at any time.
